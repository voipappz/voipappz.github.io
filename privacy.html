Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what personal data VoipAppz ("we", "us") collects when you visit voipappz.com or contact us, why we collect it and what rights you have. It does not cover the VoIP platform deployments we run for customers; those are governed by each customer agreement.

Who we are

VoipAppz is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us about anything in this policy at info@voipappz.com.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us. When you email us, we receive your name, email address, company and anything else you include. We use it to answer you, prepare a proposal and run the business relationship.
  • Usage analytics, only with your consent. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records the pages you view, how you arrived, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (country or city). IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4.
  • Technical logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address for security and to operate the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Cookies and similar technology

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Until you accept, no analytics cookies are set; Google receives only anonymous, cookieless signals (Google Consent Mode). You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

Legal bases

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for analytics, on taking steps toward a contract when you ask us about our services, and on our legitimate interest in running and securing our business for correspondence and logs. We also comply with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and its regulations.

Sharing and international transfers

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the site and the business: Google (analytics and email), GitHub (hosting). These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to answer you and, if we work together, for the duration of the relationship and the period the law requires afterwards. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email info@voipappz.com and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Israel this is the Privacy Protection Authority.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Access to the data we hold is limited to the people who need it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
