Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 23, 2026

VoipAppz wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this site. We work to meet the Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, as required by the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013.

What we have done

  • Semantic page structure with headings, landmarks and labelled navigation.
  • Full keyboard operation with a clearly visible focus indicator, and a skip link on these pages.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text contrast designed to meet WCAG AA, and text that can be enlarged to 200% without loss of content.
  • A responsive layout that works on phones, tablets and desktops, with touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels.
  • Animations kept minimal, with no flashing content.

Known limitations

Customer logos are shown as images with their company names as text alternatives. If you find any part of the site hard to use, please tell us and we will fix it or provide the information in another way.
